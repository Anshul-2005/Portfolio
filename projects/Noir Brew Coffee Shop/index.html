◆ Our Story

Crafted With
Passion, Served
With Soul.

Noir Brew was born from a singular obsession: the perfect cup of coffee. We source our beans directly from sustainable farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — roasting them in small batches to bring out their unique character.

Every drink is a ritual. Every visit, an experience. Our baristas are trained artisans who understand that coffee is more than caffeine — it's culture, craft, and connection.
